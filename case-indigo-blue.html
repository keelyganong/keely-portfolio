Indigo Blue

I've been practicing indigo dye and designing wearable art since 2020, through the evolution of my brand, Indigo Blue. In person, the work has always had a meaningful impact: through the people it connects and the relationships I've built with customers. I believe it's just as important to translate that identity into a digital experience, and to build an online community alongside a physical brand.

I care about how people experience my work in every detail. This digital archive extends that experience, bridging a physical product grounded in touch, feel, and texture with an engaging, emotional, visually designed experience that tells the Indigo Blue story.

Through it, I'm exploring how people browse, discover, and appreciate a body of work. My goal is to tell a story in digital form.
